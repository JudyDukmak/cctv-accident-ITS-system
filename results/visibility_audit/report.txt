LOW-VISIBILITY COUNT, ALL SYNTHETIC CLIPS (read only; synthetic only; no real clip, label or metadata file opened; no label, manifest, split, result or frozen file changed; nothing removed or flagged in the training data; no model, no GPU)

Signal: a colliding actor (collision[0].ids) missing from base[tick].objects = LOW VISIBILITY at that tick. The simulator drops an object when too few of its pixels are visible in the segmentation image; causes can be an occluder, the image edge, or the object being very small or far. It is called low visibility, not occlusion.
Note (expectation, not verified here): CARLA's segmentation camera labels classes, not lit pixels, so darkness alone should not drop an object; a vehicle can be in base and still be hard to see at night (seen in the box audit).
Tick rule: tick = accident_frame + annotations_start_offset == collision[0].iteration held for 2211/2211 clips (the script stops on any failure). Ticks -10/-5/+5/+10 = accident_frame -10/-5/+5/+10 frames (-0.5/-0.25/+0.25/+0.5 s at 20 fps). A tick not in the file counts as absent, never as missing.
Flags (fixed before counting): LOW_AT_IMPACT = >= 1 colliding actor missing at the collision tick; LOW_AROUND_IMPACT = >= 1 colliding actor (the same one) missing at the collision tick and at both the -5 and +5 ticks.
