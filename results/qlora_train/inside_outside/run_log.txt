6. Interpretation (exploratory; no causal claim)
  (1) Both trained epochs score much better when t* is inside window A (S 0.7433 vs 0.4274 for epoch 1 and 0.8096 vs 0.4203 for epoch 2; type accuracy 0.4101 vs 0.1077 and 0.3071 vs 0.0885), while the untrained model's S gap is small (+0.0336).
  (2) The two groups are different clips, not the same clips with a different window: the outside group has more rear-end (0.5538 vs 0.2687) and sideswipe (0.2577 vs 0.0808) and fewer head-on (0.0538 vs 0.3232), a similar night share (0.2115 vs 0.2040), and by construction a much larger classifier timing error (median |t_hat - t*| 5.96 s vs 0.30 s), so they are clips the classifier found hard to time and may be harder for other reasons too.
  (3) Much of the type-accuracy gap could come from this type mix rather than from the window: the trained models almost never name rear-end or sideswipe in either group (epoch 1 rear-end accuracy 0.0075 inside vs 0.0069 outside), and for t-bone epoch 1 is not worse outside (0.7241 vs 0.6323 inside; n 29 outside).
  (4) The S gap is present within every true type for both epochs, while the frame-blind image-centre baseline is higher outside (0.4057 vs 0.3742), so box position alone does not account for it; it is consistent with the collision being out of view, but this split cannot separate that from the clips being hard, which would need t*-centred windows for the same clips (fold-1 window B is in the manifest and cache but has no saved answers; evaluating it needs a model run).
  (5) The overall inside share 0.7558 is reproduced; fold 1 has the lowest inside share (0.6556, vs 0.7013 fold 0 and 0.9271 fold 2), so fold-1 validation numbers mix the two groups in a different proportion than the other folds would.
