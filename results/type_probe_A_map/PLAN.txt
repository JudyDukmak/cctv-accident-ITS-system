== Implementation details fixed now ==
- Per round (test map M) and per encoder k among M's clips: probe trained on the clips of the other 5 maps (all
  folds), features from encoder k, two rows per clip (t* window and frozen out-of-fold t_hat window, each the mean of
  original and flipped features), as in the first attempt; tested on the t_hat window of M's clips with val_fold k.
- Head as the first PLAN.txt: multinomial logistic regression, torch float64 L-BFGS, minimise
  C * sum_i w_i CE_i + 0.5 ||W||^2, C in {0.01, 0.1, 1, 10}, class weights balanced over the classes present,
  standardisation with the training rows' mean and SD only (also inside every inner fit). The functions are imported
  from type_probe_A.py (fit_lr, train_predict, metrics), not copied.
- Inner CV per (round, encoder): leave one training map out (5 inner folds); inner fit on both windows of the other 4
  training maps, predict the held-out map's t_hat windows; pooled inner macro accuracy per C; highest wins; ties ->
  smaller C.
- Macro accuracy = mean per-type recall over the types present in the evaluated clips (per round: the test map's
  2-3 types; pooled: 5 types).
- Majority baseline per round: the most frequent type among the clips of the 5 training maps (clip counts; ties ->
  first in alphabetical order), predicted for every clip of the test map; pooled over the 6 rounds' predictions.
  Random guess: 0.20 macro (expected value), stated, not simulated. QLoRA epoch 1: results/qlora_train/val_epoch1.csv
  on the 755 fold-1 clips, next to the probe on the same clips.
- Bootstrap: the fixed pooled out-of-fold predictions (no refit); 2,000 draws with numpy default_rng(0); each draw
  resamples the 14 scenarios with replacement (all clips of a drawn scenario, repeated as drawn); macro accuracy over
  the types present in the draw; 95% percentile interval (2.5%, 97.5%). Success = pooled macro accuracy > 0.20 AND
  the interval's lower end > 0.20.
- Outputs in results/type_probe_A_map/ (never overwritten); each run's command and output in run_log_*.txt.
